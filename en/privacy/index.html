Privacy

Last updated: November 2026.

This site

This site uses GoatCounter to count visits anonymously. GoatCounter uses no cookies and stores no personal data: it only counts page views, without identifying you or your device. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical connection data (such as the IP address) under its own policy.

The MultiSpace app

  • It asks for no account or sign-up.
  • Clones and their data stay on your phone.
  • MultiSpace does not send your clones or your data to any server.
  • The only thing it checks, at most once a day, is a public file on this site to learn whether a new version exists. You can turn it off in Settings. When requested, GitHub can see your IP address, as with any download.
  • Updates are only installed if you accept them, and Android asks you to confirm. The app verifies the file's fingerprint before installing.
  • The apps you clone connect to the internet as they normally would, with their own servers and policies.
  • Compatibility reports will only be sent if you send them, after seeing their content (feature in preparation).
