What might appear during install

Because MultiSpace is not on Google Play, Android adds warnings that do not show up with Play Store apps. That does not mean anything is wrong with the app: it is the normal security check Android and Google Play Protect run on any APK that is not from the store.

"Allow installs from this source"

The first time, your browser or file manager will ask for this permission before installing. Turn it on just for that app and continue.

"Scanning the app…" (Google Play Protect)

This is automatic: Play Protect checks any app installed outside the store before letting you continue. It is normal and does not mean anything is wrong.

"Harmful app blocked" or a similar warning

Sometimes Play Protect shows a stronger warning because MultiSpace is a new app with few installs, and because it asks for a permission to install apps and loads other apps' code: both normal for MultiSpace, but things Play Protect treats as suspicious. If you trust the source, tap "More details" and then "Install anyway".

Updates

Once installed, MultiSpace tells you inside the app when a new version is out, with the option to update or be reminded later. You do not need to come back to this page. The first time you update, it may ask for one of the permissions above again.
